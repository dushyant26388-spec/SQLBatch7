Q1. For each medical specialty, calculate the total number of doctors, their average salary (rounded to 2 decimal
places), and total salary. Show only specialties where the average salary is greater than 170,000. Sort by average
salary descending.

select specialty, count(*) as doctor_count, round(avg(salary),2) as avg_salary, sum(salary) as total_salary
from doctors group by specialty having avg(salary) > 170000 order by avg_salary desc; 


 Q2. List every doctor along with their supervisor's name. Doctors who have no supervisor must still appear in the
result with NULL shown in the supervisor column. Order by doctor ID.

select d.doctor_name, d.specialty, s.doctor_name as supervisior_name 
from doctors as d left join doctors as s on d.supervisor_id = s.doctor_id
group by specialty order by d.do0ctor_id; 



Q3. For every doctor, show their name, specialty, total number of distinct patients they treated, and the total cost of
all treatments given to their patients. Doctors with no patients must appear with 0 for both counts. Order by doctor ID.

select d.doctor_name, d.specialty, count(distinct p.patient_id) as patient_count, colaesce(sum(t.cost),0) as total_treatmment_cost
from doctors as d left join patients as p on d.doctor_id = p.doctor_id 
left join treatments as t on p.patient_id = t.patient_id
group by d.doctor_id, d.doctor_name, d.specialty
order by d.doctor_id;



Q4. Find the names, wards, and ages of all patients who received at least one treatment that cost more than
50,000. Order the results alphabetically by patient name.

select distinct p.patient_name, p.ward, p.age 
from patients as p join treatments as t on p.patient_id = t.patient_id
where t.cost > 50000 order by patient_name;
