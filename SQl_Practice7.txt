Q6. Rank all doctors by salary within their specialty from highest to lowest. Assign the same rank to doctors with
equal salaries and leave gaps in the sequence after ties. Display the doctor name, specialty, salary, and rank. Order
by specialty and rank.

select doctor_name, specialty, salary,
rank() over(partition by specialty order by salary desc) as sal_rnk
from doctors order by specialty, sal_rnk;



 Q9. Find all patients who have no treatment records at all. Show their name, ward, and admission date. Order by
patient name.

select p.patient_name, p.ward, p.admit_date 
from patients as p left join treatments as t on p.patient_id = t.patient_id
where t.patient_id is null 
order by p.patient_name; 



Q10. Find the single most expensive treatment type in each ward. If two treatment types share the same highest
cost in a ward, include both. Display the ward, treatment name, and cost. Order by ward.

select t.ward, t.treatment, t.cost from patients as p
join treatments as t on p.patient_id = t.patient_id
where t.cost=
(select max(t2.cost) from patients p2 join treatments as t2
on p2.patient_id=t2.patient_id where p2.ward=p.ward)
order by p.ward;
